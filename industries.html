OUR TARGET ECOSYSTEM

THE HEAVYWEIGHTS

PathVision is designed for a connected mobility ecosystem involving fleet operators, transport authorities, infrastructure planners, and safety-focused organizations. The system enhances visibility, safety, and operational awareness across heavy vehicle environments. It enables better monitoring, decision-making, and system-level insights across transportation networks.

Used in logistics and supply operations, these vehicles require continuous monitoring of driver behavior and road conditions. PathVision provides real-time visibility into driving patterns, road risks, and operational safety.

  • Operational safety visibility & threat log integration
