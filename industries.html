THE HEAVYWEIGHTS

PathVision is exclusively engineered for the complexities of heavy mobility. Our AI does not deal with passenger cars. Use the dashboard below to explore the specific heavy vehicles we protect and the core industries we transform.

RECALIBRATING MATRIX...

Commercial Freight Trucks

Multi-axle rigid trucks and articulated tractor-trailers navigating national highways. Susceptible to blind-spot collisions, highway hypnosis, and aerodynamic drag inefficiencies.

Primary Risk: Rear-end Collision Focus: Fatigue Detection
🚌

Public & Private Buses

Intracity transit buses and interstate sleeper coaches. Operating in dense urban environments with high pedestrian density and significant passenger liability requirements.

Primary Risk: Urban Blind Spots Focus: 360° Surround View
🚧

Heavy Construction Equipment

Dump trucks, transit mixers, and earthmovers. Operating in off-road, high-dust environments with severe vibration profiles and complex reverse-maneuvering challenges.

Primary Risk: Reversing Accidents Focus: Ultrasonic Integration
🛢️

Hazardous Material Tankers

Liquid and gas carriers requiring extreme stabilization monitoring. High center of gravity makes them prone to rollover events during harsh cornering or evasive maneuvers.

Primary Risk: Rollover Events Focus: 6-Axis IMU Telemetry

Logistics & Supply Chain

Goal: OPEX Reduction Deployment: Retrofit Kits

Goal: Passenger Safety Standard Deployment: Fleet-Wide Integration
