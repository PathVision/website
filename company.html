WHO WE ARE

Built in India,
For India

PathVision Innovations Pvt. Ltd. was founded in 2021 with a single conviction: India's heavy vehicle ecosystem deserves intelligent safety technology designed specifically for its roads, weather, and traffic conditions — not adapted from solutions built for European motorways.

Our team of 5+ engineers, data scientists, and domain experts — drawn from IIIT and GITAM — is building IMAS, the Integrated Mobility Assistance System. It is a full-stack AI safety and intelligence platform that goes from sensor to insight in under 100 milliseconds, retrofittable to any existing vehicle in 3 days.

Bootstrapped and operating from Bengaluru, we are active across 2 states and emerging every quarter.

OUR JOURNEY

From Idea to
Testing in Vehicles

Four years of focused building — idea to prototype on field, one milestone at a time.

2021: Q4

Idea Spark

IMAS began as a response to a very real problem: unsafe driving conditions and lack of intelligent assistance in Indian heavy vehicles. Early exploration started with understanding accident patterns, driver behavior, and road conditions in India.

Day One
2022: Q1-Q3

Discovery Begins

Instead of jumping into building, the focus shifted to understanding the ecosystem deeply. Conversations with drivers, fleet operators, and stakeholders revealed critical pain points, behavioral patterns, and on-ground constraints.

2022: Q4

Early Validation

The solution received early external validation for its feasibility and impact potential. Recognized for Best Viability by Young Indians (Yi) & CII

First Validation
2023

Development Phase

Transition from insights to engineering. Core system architecture defined and development initiated across hardware and AI components. The first working hardware prototype of IMAS was developed, marking the shift from concept to tangible system. Runner-up – Innovation Oasis, IIIT Nagpur

First Prototype
2024 — Q1

National Recognition

IMAS gained strong recognition at a national-level innovation platform. Winner – AI Track, Empresario 2024 (IIT Kharagpur)

National Winner
2025: Q1

Official Beginning

PathVision Innovations was formally registered, transitioning from a project into a structured startup entity.

Incorporated
2025: Q4

Into the Real World

Prototype deployed for real-world testing in vehicles. System evaluated under actual driving conditions to measure performance, reliability, and adaptability.

2026

Prototype → Product

Current focus is on transforming the prototype into a production-ready system
